About

My work is about bridging gaps: making European legislation make sense for regional priorities, or bringing international human rights commitments down to the local level.

Over the years, this has looked like:

  • Policy & Advocacy, Brussels: EU legislative monitoring, Packaging and Packaging Waste Directive, briefings for regional decision-makers, Canary Islands Delegation to the EU.
  • Research, Italy: PhD, Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna, Summa Cum Laude, translating global rights standards into local practice.
  • Finance & Audit: reading between the lines, questioning data, finding clarity in detail.

I work across Spanish, English, Italian, French and German, which keeps me precise moving between institutions, research networks and advocacy spaces.
